# ECCLISIA

## Problem Statement Details

- **PS ID:** `ECCLISIA26-001`
- **PS Title:** AI-Powered Multilingual Voice Assistant for Digital Form Automation

---

### Problem Statement Description
People with limited digital literacy often struggle to complete online forms because of language barriers and complex form fields. Develop an AI-powered application that accepts voice input in multiple languages, extracts relevant information, automatically fills appropriate form fields, and allows users to review and correct the information before submission.

---

### Technical Requirements
1. **Voice Input:** Voice input in at least two languages.
2. **Speech-to-Text & Extraction:** Speech-to-text conversion and information extraction.
3. **Field Mapping:** Automatic mapping of information to form fields.
4. **Validation:** Detection of missing or invalid information.
5. **Review & Verification:** Form review and correction before submission.

---

### Expected Outcome
A working prototype that completes digital forms using multilingual voice input.
